# Acceptable use policy

> **DRAFT — requires legal review before use.**

Use ScoreCode to manage and publish your own institution's results. Do not:
- put in data you have no right to process, or other institutions' data;
- try to see another student's or another institution's data, test our security without written
  permission, or get around limits, rate limits or access controls;
- upload malware, or files designed to harm others;
- use ScoreCode to harass, discriminate against or publicly rank children by name;
- resell or share access to your account outside your institution;
- send students messages unrelated to their studies through ScoreCode.

Found a security problem? Tell us at [security contact]; we won't take action against good-faith reports.
We may suspend accounts that break this policy, after notice except in an emergency, and we tell you why.
