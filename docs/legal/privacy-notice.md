# Privacy notice

> **DRAFT — requires legal review before use.** Not in force. `[Bracketed]` items are for the owner.

## Who we are, and for whom we act
ScoreCode, run by [legal name], [address]. For **students' and parents' data**, your **school or
institution decides** what is collected and why; ScoreCode processes it on the institution's behalf
(as its *data processor*). Questions about your results or records: ask your institution first. For
**staff accounts, our customers' contacts and visitors to our website**, ScoreCode itself decides
(as *data fiduciary*).

## What we process
| Who | Data | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Students (for the institution) | Name, enrollment number, class and section, date of birth if the institution adds it, guardian name, email and phone if given, marks and results, marksheets | To compute, publish and verify results for the institution |
| Staff | Name, email, role, sign-in records | To run the institution's account |
| Everyone signing in | Address (IP), browser, time of each session | Security and the law's record-keeping (kept one year) |
| Visitors to verification pages | The code entered, time | To show whether a marksheet is genuine |

We **don't** sell data, show advertising, profile students, or track children's behaviour.

## Who else receives data (processors we use)
Hosting in India ([Hostinger, Mumbai]); file storage ([Cloudflare R2, region]); encrypted backups
([Amazon S3, Mumbai]); email delivery (Brevo: the recipient's address and the message); error reports
(Sentry: technical errors only, with personal data removed before they leave our servers);
Cloudflare (network and security). Each is bound by contract to process data only for us.

## How long we keep it
- Your institution's data: while it uses ScoreCode. If it closes its account, 30 days later everything
  is erased (backups expire within about a month), except billing records the law requires.
- Records of processing (sign-ins, emails sent, background jobs): one year, then deleted.
- Copies of uploaded spreadsheets: 30 days after the import.

## Security
Encryption in transit; passwords hashed; two-step sign-in for staff; each institution's data kept
separate in the database; encrypted, tested backups; an audit log of every change.

## Your rights
You can ask for access, correction or erasure of your data, nominate someone to act for you, and complain.
For students' data your institution answers (we help it within [7] days); we answer within 90 days at the
latest. Contact: [grievance officer], see [grievance](grievance.md). You may also complain to the Data
Protection Board of India.

## Children
Students' accounts are created by their institution, which is responsible for any parental consent the
law requires. We use children's data only to provide their results.

## Changes
We will post changes here and tell institutions' administrators [30] days before they take effect.
