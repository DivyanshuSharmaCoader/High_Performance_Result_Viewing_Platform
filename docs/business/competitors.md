# Competitors and their prices (P18.13)

Accessed **2026-10-10**. Each row says where the price comes from. **Official** means the vendor's own
page or documentation; **secondary** means a listing site (Capterra, Techjockey, a review blog), recorded
only when the official page publishes nothing, and marked as uncertain. No price here was guessed. A
blank means the vendor doesn't publish one; ask for a quote before comparing.

ScoreCode is a **results tool** (exams → marks → results → publication → verifiable marksheets), not a
full school ERP (fees, attendance, transport, HR). The comparison is split accordingly: schools that
already run an ERP rarely replace it for results; schools without one compare us with spreadsheets,
results-only tools and the cheapest ERPs.

## 3. Doing it by hand

Spreadsheets plus WhatsApp groups or printed lists. No licence cost; the cost is staff hours, mistakes,
and students' results visible to the whole group. This is a hypothesis about the most common "competitor"
for small schools, to be checked in discovery calls; we have no survey data.

## 4. What this means for ScoreCode (to validate, not facts)

- **Price floor.** The cheapest full ERP with results found is VidyalayaERP Premium at ₹13,500/yr
  (secondary source), and eSchoolApp at ₹50/student (₹7,500 for 150 students, ₹30,000 for 600).
  ScoreCode's Starter (₹7,999/yr up to 600 students, about ₹13/student at the top of the band) is below
  both, for less scope.
- **Where ScoreCode is different, by design:** no roll-number-plus-date-of-birth lookup (students activate
  with a per-student code, so results can't be enumerated); corrections that keep the live version and
  tell only affected students; QR-verifiable marksheets; Hindi and Punjabi for parents; a data export with
  no lock-in. Whether schools pay for these is the open question.
- **Not differentiated:** report-card templates (every ERP has them), CSV import (GradeCloud has it).
