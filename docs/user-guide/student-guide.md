# Student guide

## Activate your account (once)
1. You get an **activation slip** from your institution, with your enrollment number and a one-time
   code.
2. Go to your institution's ScoreCode address and choose **Activate**.
3. Enter your enrollment number and the code, and choose a password.

If the code has expired, ask your institution for a new slip. ScoreCode staff can't issue one.

## Your results
- After signing in, you see your **latest result**:
  - the outcome (passed, or what's pending);
  - your percentage, or **SGPA** and grades, depending on your institution;
  - every paper with its components.
- **Earlier results** lists everything published to you, across academic years.
- **Class statistics** (if your institution shows them): the average, and how the class did per paper.
  You never see another student's marks.
- New results arrive with a **notification** (the bell), and by email if you've kept emails on (Account
  → Notifications).
- **Language:** English, हिन्दी or ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, from the menu at the top. Your institution picks the default;
  your choice is kept on your phone. (Notifications and the sign-in pages are in English.)

## Your marksheet (PDF)
On a result, **Download** gets the official marksheet. Under it are its **verification code** and a
verify link. Share those with anyone who needs to check the marksheet: an employer, a university, an
embassy.

## Checking someone's marksheet (no account needed)
- Scan the QR code on the marksheet, or go to `verify.<scorecode domain>` and type the 26-character
  code.
- **Valid** means the institution issued it and it is current. You'll see the institution, the exam,
  the issue date, a masked name and enrollment number, and the PDF's fingerprint (SHA-256).
  - To be sure the file you were given is the original, compare that fingerprint with the file's.
- **Superseded** means a revised result was issued: ask for the latest marksheet.
- **Withdrawn** or **Revoked** means it is no longer valid.

## Keeping your account safe
- Use a password you don't use anywhere else. ScoreCode refuses passwords known from data breaches.
- **Account → Sign out everywhere** if you signed in on a shared computer.
- ScoreCode will never ask for your password by email or phone.
