# Faculty guide

You enter marks for the papers and sections your exam office assigned to you. You don't see anyone
else's.

## Sign in
Use the invitation email to set your password, at your institution's address. Then turn on **two-step
sign-in** (Account → Two-step sign-in) with an authenticator app, and keep the recovery codes.

## Your work list (Marks entry)
Each row is one paper for one section, with its deadline and status: *Open*, *Submitted*, or *Returned*
(the exam office sent it back with a reason).

## Entering marks
- Open a row to get the **mark sheet**: one line per student, a column per component (e.g. *Internal /
  40*, *End semester / 60*).
- **Keys:** Enter or ↓ moves down, ↑ moves up. You can paste a block of cells straight from Excel.
- **It saves as you type.** A mark above the maximum, or something that isn't a number, is flagged on
  the line and not saved.
- **Attendance codes** instead of marks: `AB` absent, `EX` exempt, `MP` malpractice, `WH` withheld. A
  code next to marks is flagged rather than overwriting them.
- **If a colleague changes the same mark** at the same time, you'll see their value and can choose.
  Nothing is overwritten silently.

## Importing from Excel
On the mark sheet, use **Import from Excel**:
1. Download the template. It's pre-filled with your section's students and has the right columns and
   allowed ranges.
2. Fill it in and upload it.
3. The check lists every problem before anything is saved. Fix them and upload again.

## Submitting
When the sheet is complete, use **Submit**. The exam office can see it's ready, and you can no longer
edit it unless they return it to you.
